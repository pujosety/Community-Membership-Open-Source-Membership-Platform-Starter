 Community Membership

> Open-source community membership platform built with **Flutter** and **Laravel**. Membership cards, events, chapters, social feed, QR verification, and a scalable REST API.

`Community-Membership-Open-Source-Membership-Platform-Starter` is for organizations, associations, and communities that want digital membership management from one codebase — loyalty, identity, events, and governance included.

---

## Why this matters

Community identity is usually split across tools. WhatsApp lists, shared spreadsheets, QR checklists, and manual onboarding become hard to maintain quickly.

This repo replaces that mess with a unified system: digital membership cards, event management, chapter-based organization, a social-style feed, QR verification, notifications, and a REST API.

---

## What you get

- Digital membership cards and profiles
- Event and chapter management
- Social feed for announcements and discussions
- QR verification for member status
- Notifications
- REST API with scalable structure
- Docker-ready setup
- OpenAPI reference
- Documentation-first approach
